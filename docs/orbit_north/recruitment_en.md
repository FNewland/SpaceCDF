# ORBIT NORTH

**Design a CubeSat mission. Command a live mission-control simulation.
Build a career in space — in a 12-day intensive program.**

**6 – 21 August 2026 · University of Ottawa · uOttawa Cyberrange Mission
Control Facility**

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **12-day intensive program** | 6 – 21 August 2026, weekdays, 09:00 – 17:00, in person |
| **Mission · end-to-end** | from Canadian context to operator console |
| **2 codes · UG and Grad** | GNG 3100 (UG) · SYS 5186 (Grad) |
| **Prior space experience** | not needed |

ORBIT NORTH takes you from the Canadian space ecosystem and its
regulatory environment, through a full preliminary CubeSat design in a
Concurrent Design Facility, to the seat of a mission-control console —
in twelve in-person days at the University of Ottawa. Built for
students with the drive to do something real.

---

## What you'll do

### Week 1 — Canadian context, CDF intro & concept development  ·  6 – 7 August 2026

Two days that ground you in the Canadian space ecosystem and put you
straight to work. Thursday morning covers the past, present, and
emerging Canadian sector; Thursday afternoon brings a short regulatory
teaser and your first session in the SpaceCDF tool. Friday is mission
architecture and concept work in the CDF, with the team's risk
register opened at end of day.

`Canadian sector`  ·  `SpaceCDF`  ·  `Mission concept`  ·  `Risk register`

### Week 2 — MCR, regulatory & CDF intensive  ·  10 – 14 August 2026

Monday morning is the **Mission Concept Review**; Monday afternoon
covers the Canadian regulatory landscape — RSSSA, ITU/ISED spectrum
coordination, export controls, debris mitigation, registration. The
remainder of the week is the SpaceCDF intensive: requirements
baseline, power, AOCS and thermal, communications, structure and
propulsion, integration, V&V, cost, and risk closure — converging on
a **Preliminary Design Review** on Friday afternoon.

`MCR`  ·  `RSSSA · ISED`  ·  `Subsystem design`  ·  `Trade studies`  ·  `PDR`

### Week 3 — Operations & mission simulation  ·  17 – 21 August 2026

Two days of spacecraft operations training at the uOttawa Cyberrange —
ground systems, flight dynamics, mission-control system, planning
tool, procedures. Then two days running a live simulation: take the
operator seat while another team observes and documents, then swap.
Work nominal operations and inject contingency in real time. Closes
with a structured debrief and final mission review.

`Ground systems`  ·  `Flight dynamics`  ·  `Anomaly response`  ·  `Mission debrief`

---

## What you'll be able to do

- Situate a mission concept in the Canadian space ecosystem.
- Navigate Canadian satellite licensing, export controls, and RSSSA
  obligations.
- Take a discipline role in a Concurrent Design Facility and deliver a
  complete preliminary CubeSat mission across all principal subsystems.
- Maintain a risk register and a planning baseline through the design
  week.
- Execute spacecraft operations procedures in a mission-control
  environment.
- Respond to anomalies and contingency scenarios in a live simulation.
