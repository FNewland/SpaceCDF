# Peer-Review Worksheet Pack

You are the observer team. While the operator team flies the shift,
**you build the evidence** the cohort uses at the post-sim debrief.
Each worksheet below is a single page, used once per observed
shift. Use the worksheet for the axis the instructor assigns you;
do not try to fill in all four for the same shift.

The four axes:

1. **Team** — role clarity, FD authority, coverage, fatigue.
2. **Communications** — voice loop, callouts, handover, MCS chat.
3. **Space usage** — console layout, line of sight, distractions,
   physical comfort.
4. **Technical** — procedure adherence, anomaly response timing,
   telemetry interpretation, command discipline.

---

## How to use a worksheet

Each worksheet has four blocks:

| Block | What to write |
|-------|---------------|
| **Observed** | Facts. Timestamps, callouts, telemetry values, exact words. **No judgement here.** |
| **Worked well** | One or two specific behaviours that improved the shift. |
| **Could improve** | One or two specific behaviours that hurt the shift. |
| **Specific recommendation** | What you'd change *concretely* for next time. One sentence, actionable. |

Rules:

- Be specific. "Team didn't communicate" fails. "At T+34 the AOCS
  position commanded a wheel spin without an FD GO" passes.
- Use timestamps. The simulator clock is on every screen.
- Read the worksheet **after** the shift. Do not show your draft
  to the operator team during the shift.
- The instructor reads worksheets at the debrief. Be direct. The
  goal is to make the operator team better, not to be polite.

## Worksheet rotation

Each pass, observer team uses **one** worksheet axis. Suggested
rotation across the simulation week:

| Day | Pass | Observer team uses |
|-----|------|--------------------|
| Wed (LEOP) | Pass 1 | Communications |
| Wed (LEOP) | Pass 2 | Technical |
| Thu (Commissioning) | Pass-set 1 | Team |
| Thu (Commissioning) | Pass-set 2 | Space usage |

This way every axis gets fresh observation across the week. At the
Friday wrap, each team holds two completed worksheets — those go
into the cohort lessons-learned register.

---

## After the shift

1. Take 10 minutes alone with the worksheet before the debrief
   starts. Re-read what you wrote; tidy it.
2. At the debrief, the FD speaks first. Then your team reads its
   worksheet aloud. The operator team responds with one *accept*
   and one *defend* per axis.
3. Hand the worksheet to the instructor at end of debrief.
