1. GEOMETRIC MATHEMATICAL MODEL (GMM)
   - Node definitions: one node per major component + structure panels + radiators
   - Surface optical properties (absorptivity α, emissivity ε) from material database
   - View factors between internal nodes (estimated from geometry)
   - External view factors to space, Earth (albedo + IR), Sun

2. THERMAL MATHEMATICAL MODEL (TMM)
   - Conductive links (component-to-panel, panel-to-panel)

   - Eclipse transient (step function on solar flux)

3. ANALYSIS CASES
   - Hot case: minimum eclipse, maximum internal dissipation, EOL optical properties
   - Cold case: maximum eclipse, minimum dissipation (safe mode), BOL optical properties
   - Transient: temperature evolution through one orbit (hot and cold cases)

- thermal/solver.py — Steady-state (Newton-Raphson) and transient (RK45) solvers

- thermal/run_analysis.py — Execute hot/cold/transient cases, generate temperature report
- thermal/plot.py — Temperature vs time plots, node temperature bar charts
