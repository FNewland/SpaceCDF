2. COST BREAKDOWN STRUCTURE (CBS)

   - RDT&E (Phase A-D): design, development, test, integration

   - Launch services
   - Ground segment (development + 1 year operations)
   - Program management, systems engineering, mission assurance (wrap rates)

   - Point estimate (most likely)

   - Pessimistic (+50% for low-TRL, +30% for high-TRL)

   - Key cost drivers and sensitivities
